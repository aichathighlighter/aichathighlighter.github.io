How it works

Nothing floats on the page until you select text.

  1. Select text

    Drag over any part of an AI answer. A small row of colors appears next to it.

  2. Pick a color

    Click a color, press Alt+H, or right-click. Click a new color to change it, or the slashed chip to clear it.

  3. Come back anytime

    Reopen that chat and your highlights are still there, even after you restart your computer.
