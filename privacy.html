AI Chat Highlighter

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

AI Chat Highlighter is a Chrome extension for highlighting text in AI chat websites. It saves your highlights on the computer where you make them. The developer does not receive your highlights, your chats, or your browsing history.

What is saved, and where

When you highlight a passage, the extension saves, in Chrome on that computer only:

  • The passage you chose
  • Its color
  • The time you highlighted it
  • The chat’s name, site, and address, so the highlight can be shown again in that chat

The extension also remembers your chosen color, whether the color chips are showing, and whether you have Pro. It asks for your agreement before it saves your first highlight.

It does not save or send your full chat, and it never reads your passwords, your card details, or the history of other sites you visit. To show a highlight again, it looks for that passage on the chat page inside your browser; nothing leaves your computer.

It runs only on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Copilot, DeepSeek, NotebookLM, and Meta AI. It does not run on any other site unless you choose Enable highlighting on this site for that site.

AI summaries

If you have Pro and click Summarize with AI, Chrome’s built-in AI writes the summary on your computer. Your highlights are not sent to us or to any AI service. The first time, Chrome may download its AI model from Google; that download does not include your highlights. Computers that can’t run Chrome’s built-in AI get a key-points list made on the computer instead.

How your information is used

Saved text is used only to show your highlights, group them by chat, and summarize them. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to decide credit or lending.

The use of information received from you stays within the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Payments for Lifetime Pro

Highlighting is free. Lifetime Pro is a one-time payment. Payments are processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay, a payment service for browser extensions.

  • You enter your email and card on Stripe’s secure checkout page. The extension and the developer never see your card number.
  • Stripe and ExtensionPay keep your email and payment record so they can send your receipt, confirm that you paid, and let you restore Pro on another computer by signing in with your email.
  • To check whether Pro is unlocked, the extension contacts ExtensionPay using a random ID created for your copy of the extension. It does not send your highlights.
  • The developer can see buyers’ emails and payment records in Stripe and ExtensionPay, and uses them only for receipts, refunds, and support.

Stripe’s and ExtensionPay’s own privacy policies cover the information they handle.

How long it stays

Highlights stay until you delete them in Pro, remove the extension, or clear the extension’s data in Chrome. Closing the browser does not delete them. They do not follow you to another computer. Payment records are kept by Stripe and ExtensionPay as their policies and the law require.

Children

AI Chat Highlighter is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect their information.
